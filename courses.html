About the Program

The Path to Becoming a Herbal Physician Begins Here

The Mount Kailash School of Esoteric Knowledge presents the Level 1 Herbal Physician Course — a rigorous, comprehensive program that equips you with the knowledge, skills, and clinical understanding to practice herbal medicine with authority and confidence.

This program blends the wisdom of traditional herbal medicine with modern scientific understanding, covering everything from ethnobotany and pharmacognosy to anatomy, pathology, and the legal frameworks governing herbal practice.
